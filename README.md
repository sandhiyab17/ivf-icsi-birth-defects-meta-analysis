# Birth Defects in Children Conceived by IVF and ICSI: A Meta-Analysis
A meta-analysis and evaluation of birth defect risks in children conceived via Assisted Reproductive Technologies (IVF vs. ICSI) compared to spontaneous conception.

A systematic evaluation and meta-analysis assessing the association between Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART)—specifically In Vitro Fertilization (IVF) and Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI)—and the risk of birth defects compared to spontaneously conceived (SC) children.

Presented by: **Sandhiya B**

---

##  Abstract & Overview
Since the birth of the first IVF-conceived child in 1978, over 1 million babies worldwide have been born using ART. This project analyzes published evidence to evaluate whether infants conceived via ART face an elevated risk of birth defects, and assesses if there is a risk difference between IVF and ICSI.

---

##  Key Concepts & Technologies

### Assisted Reproductive Technologies (ART)
- **IVF (In Vitro Fertilization):** Eggs are removed from the ovary, combined with sperm outside the body to form embryos, cultured, and transferred to the uterus or cryopreserved.
- **ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection):** A single selected sperm cell is manually injected directly into an egg. Commonly used for severe male infertility factors.

---

##  Study Structure & Meta-Analysis Data

The meta-analysis evaluated evidence across **population-based** and **clinic-based** studies:

- **ART vs. Spontaneously Conceived (SC):**
  - Evaluated **46 out of 56 studies** involving **124,468 infants**.
  - **Overall Relative Risk (RR):** `1.37` (95% CI), showing a statistically significant increased risk of birth defects in ART-conceived infants compared to SC children.
  - Subgroup analysis revealed a notable increased risk in the **nervous system** (RR = `2.01`, 95% CI).

- **IVF vs. ICSI Comparison:**
  - Evaluated **24 studies** involving **46,890 IVF infants** and **27,754 ICSI infants**.
  - **Relative Risk (RR):** `1.05`, indicating **no significant overall risk difference** between IVF and ICSI.

---

##  Discussion & Risk Factors

The observed increased risk of birth defects in ART-conceived infants may be influenced by underlying parental infertility factors rather than the procedures themselves.

Key confounding factors include:
- Parental age and duration of infertility
- Underlying causes of maternal or paternal infertility
- Environmental exposures and health behaviors (e.g., alcohol, smoking)
- Specific ART laboratory procedures
